How much Trick Room is worth depends on the archetype. In the 2v2 endgame sim (your back two against the pairs their archetype has on the field at turn 3, Trick Room up for 2–4 turns or not at all), it changes your win share by: Sand +43; Balance +37; Snow +30; Tailwind +29; Trick Room teams +19; Rain + Sun +18; Sun +16; Perish +13; Rain +12; Indeedee TR -3 points. The lead scores use those ratios, so Trick Room counts more against fast teams and less against slow ones.

All 15 lead pairs were screened against each archetype’s six most common opposing leads in 5,000 Showdown M-C Bo3 replays; the leads near the top were re-run with 12 RNG samples per cell in Showdown’s engine with Champions mechanics and scored with a win model fit on 7,781 games. Speed under Trick Room

Lowest moves first. Your six are shaded; the others are the most common opposing spreads on the Showdown ladder (Smogon’s August stats, the latest published) and in tournament sets, with the Speed points each spread uses. Equal numbers are a coin flip.

Nothing common sits at 89, or between 64 and 69. One Speed point on Blastoise (89) only changes the two 88s, Quiet Mega Blastoise and Sylveon’s most common spread: you move before them without Trick Room and after them under it, instead of a coin flip; the usual Mega Blastoise is at 130 either way. Your Brave Kingambit (63) only changes the mirror: under Trick Room it moves before every Adamant Kingambit instead of tying the 70s, and without Trick Room it moves after the 70s instead of tying them. Sylveon’s spreads are scattered from 80 to 103.

Sets. Spreads are the most common ones in Showdown’s Reg M-B Bo3 ladder stats (Smogon, August 2026) and MetaVGC’s tournament and ladder data (Indeedee, Farigiraf, Kingambit, Torkoal). Blastoise’s nature follows the build with exactly its four moves on Pokémon Zone. Incineroar’s set is the most common one on Indeedee Trick Room teams in the 5,000 M-C Bo3 replays the leads were simulated from. The two spare stat points go wherever the top spread put them; they change nothing that matters.
